# TV check: plan 3 (heating, camera and layout)

- **Date:** 23 November 2026
- **TV:** LG C9, webOS 4.5, app version 0.3.0
- **Home Assistant:** Simon's own, 2026.11.2
- **Tried by:** Simon, who reported the results in conversation.

## Result

Everything worked (heating, reorganising, the Back key, signing in) except the Ring doorbell's picture,
which stayed old.

## The doorbell picture

- Home Assistant's own card for the Ring camera showed the same old picture. Opening its live view showed
  video; afterwards the still changed, and the TV showed the new still too.
- So the still is Ring's last snapshot. Ring sends a new one only after an event (motion, a ring) or a live
  view, and the app can only show what Home Assistant has. Live video stays out of the first version.
- The final review also found that a slow picture (Ring's come through its cloud) could be thrown away by
  the next refresh, so a newer still might never appear. Fixed in `928f720` and installed the same day.
