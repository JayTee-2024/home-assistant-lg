# TV check: plan 6 (live camera view and the Back key)

- **Date:** 23 November 2026
- **TV:** LG C9, webOS 4.5 (Chromium 53.0.2785.34), app version 1.1.0
- **Home Assistant:** Simon's own, with a Ring doorbell
- **Tried by:** Simon, who reported the results in conversation: "It all looks good and working as expected."

## Before building: the throwaway test

A throwaway test on the TV showed Home Assistant offers the Ring doorbell live over WebRTC only, and the
TV plays it (720×720 H264 with Opus sound) through `webkitRTCPeerConnection`. The first attempt failed
because its offer included a data channel: Ring answered with audio and video only, and the TV refused an
answer with fewer channels than its offer. Without the data channel it played.

## Result

- The camera opens full screen, with the picture filling the height and the bar across the top.
- Watch live plays the doorbell live; Sound on/off, Stop live and Back work, and closing ends the session.
- The Home button while live stops the sound.
- Back on the sign-in screen steps back a form; on the address form it leaves the app; with the TV
  keyboard open it only closes the keyboard.
