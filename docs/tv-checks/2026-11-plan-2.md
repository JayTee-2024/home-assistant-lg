# TV check: plan 2 (sign in and control devices)

- **Date:** 23 November 2026
- **TV:** LG OLED65C9PLA (C9), webOS 4.5, rooted, app version 0.2.0 installed with `npm run deploy`
- **Home Assistant:** Simon's own, 2026.11.2, at `192.168.1.100:8123`
- **Tried by:** Simon, with his own account. He reported the results in conversation.

## What was tried

Signing in, adding devices in Settings, moving around the home screen with the arrows, switching a light
with OK, holding OK to open a panel, changing brightness, closing and reopening the app, and signing out.

## Result

Simon: "worked perfectly", with one exception.

- **Found:** after the first address failed, "Could not reach Home Assistant" stayed on screen even once a
  valid address had been entered and the username and password form had appeared. Cause: moving to that
  form kept the previous message. Fixed in `62f28ef` and installed on the TV the same day.

## Not answered by this run

  device, entity and area lists. (The device list command is not admin-only in Home Assistant's own code;
  the other two have not been checked.)
- Whether Home Assistant 2026.9's "child devices" appear, and are grouped sensibly.
- The codes for Back, the colour keys and the channel keys (plan 1 left these to "one more look"). Back works
  in the app, which shows its code is right.
